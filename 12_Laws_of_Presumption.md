# 📜 The 12 Presumptions of Law

> These 12 foundational presumptions are commonly used in court systems around the world—especially those modeled on Roman or maritime law. When not rebutted, they stand as **facts in law**, even if they are false. Knowing these is essential for asserting your position as the sovereign owner of your IP infrastructure and digital rights.

## ⚖️ 1. **Presumption of Public Record**
If something is recorded publicly and remains unchallenged, it is presumed **true** and **lawful**.  
*→ Action: Timestamp and publicly post your IP, claims, and preservation records.*

## ⚖️ 2. **Presumption of Public Service**
You are presumed to be a public servant or agent unless you rebut this and establish your **sovereign standing**.  
*→ Action: Explicitly declare private ownership of your systems and non-agency status in all filings.*

## ⚖️ 3. **Presumption of Public Oath**
You are presumed to have sworn allegiance to the corporate state unless you **object and declare otherwise.**

## ⚖️ 4. **Presumption of Immunity**
Court agents are presumed to have immunity unless it is directly **challenged** and placed on the record.

---

## ⚖️ 5. **Presumption of Summons**
If a summons or notice is sent and not rebutted, it is presumed you accept all terms therein.  
*→ Action: Always respond in writing and on record.*

## ⚖️ 7. **Presumption of Court Agent**

## ⚖️ 8. **Presumption of Ownership**
The court presumes the state owns all property unless you prove **true and original title**.

## ⚖️ 9. **Presumption of Knowledge**
If you do not object or rebut a statement, it is presumed you **agree** and have full knowledge.

If you use a platform, tool, or service without stating otherwise, the system assumes you agree to all terms.
